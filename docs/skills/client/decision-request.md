Turn open points that need a product/client decision into closed questions — each researched in the codebase, with options, consequences, effort and a default — tracked in a decision log until answered. Use when findings, tickets or a plan contain "needs discussion" / "ask the client" items, or when the client's answers arrive and must be recorded and turned into follow-up work.
